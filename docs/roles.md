# BPI — Co-founder roles & responsibilities (internal)

> Internal working doc. NOT published on the website. Lightweight by design —
> ownership means "who has the final call and is accountable," not rigid silos.
> In a two-person shop, both of us touch everything.

**The through-line:** Kamal owns *why it's true and why it matters* (research →
content → credibility). Saqeer owns *who needs to hear it and how it lands*
(legal soundness → relationships → engagement). We jointly own direction and
the guardrails.

| Area | Owner | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy & positioning | **Joint** | What BPI stands for, which issues to prioritise, which opportunities to chase |
| Governance, structure & red lines | **Joint** (Saqeer drafts) | The guardrails — incl. "trusted-not-useful-to-industry" and the think-tank/advisory firewall |
| Partnerships & network | **Joint** | Universities, think tanks, industry, community — the doors, opened by both |
| Fundraising | **Joint** | Saqeer's relationships + Kamal's narrative and materials |
| Research & frameworks | **Kamal** | Papers, taxonomy, tax, classification — the analytical core |
| Writing, publications & thought leadership | **Kamal** | The public record that builds credibility |
| Public education, media & comms | **Kamal** (Saqeer supports) | Explaining Bitcoin clearly; the public voice on substance |
| Legal & regulatory analysis | **Saqeer** | Making sure positions are legally sound and framed right |
| Government & institutional engagement | **Saqeer** | Relationships, meetings, stakeholder consultations, on-ground execution |
| Entity, compliance & operations | **Saqeer** (lawyer) | Registration, filings, the boring-but-critical plumbing |

## Two things to protect together

1. **The firewall.** Engagement is our biggest asset *and* our biggest
   credibility risk. The moment it reads as "access for hire," the
   "trusted by policymakers" positioning breaks. Both of us own that red line.
2. **Keep it lightweight.** Don't over-formalise. This doc is enough until
   there's a third person.
