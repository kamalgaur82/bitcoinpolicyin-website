## Commitment

8–12 hours a week for Fellows, 4–6 for Associate Fellows. Not full-time — but not casual.

## Three tiers

- **Associate Fellow** — 4–6 hrs/week — A training track for students, early-stage contributors, and high-potential volunteers.
- **Fellow** — 8–12 hrs/week — The core engine — the primary producer of research, explainers, policy briefs, and workshops.
- **Senior Fellow** — Project-based — A domain expert leading major research, institutional collaborations, and mentoring.

## The 12-week cycle

- **Weeks 1–2** — Orientation, research training, and project assignment.
- **Weeks 3–4** — Foundational reading, framing sessions, and first drafts.
- **Weeks 5–9** — Deep research, writing, and policy analysis.
- **Weeks 10–11** — Reviews, editing, and public-education output.
- **Week 12** — Final presentations, publication, and graduation.

## What every fellow produces

- A 10–15 page research paper
- A 1,000–2,500 word policy brief
- A public explainer (article, deck, or video)
- A workshop or lecture
- A capstone project

## What the fellowship teaches

1. Bitcoin foundations, in an India context
2. Research & writing discipline
3. Policy thinking & comparative frameworks
4. Public education & clear communication
5. Institutional conduct & integrity

## How we select

Skill can be trained; temperament and integrity can't. We look for:

- Humility over ego
- Consistency over brilliance
- Curiosity over credentials
- India-first, not personality-first

Values: humility, independence, discipline, evidence, consistency, clarity
